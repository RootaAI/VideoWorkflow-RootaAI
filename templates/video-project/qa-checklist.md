# Video Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Brief, script, and storyboard were approved.
- [ ] Every shot has a clear purpose.
- [ ] External assets have complete provenance and usage records.
- [ ] Opening communicates the promise within 1–3 seconds.
- [ ] Mobile crop, safe zones, text size, and subtitle wrapping were visually checked.
- [ ] Narration sounds natural and subtitle timing follows speech.
- [ ] Music supports rather than masks narration.
- [ ] Claims match what the video actually demonstrates.
- [ ] Opening, middle, transitions, subtitle-heavy scenes, and ending were frame-checked.
- [ ] The full video was watched with sound.
- [ ] `ffprobe` confirms the approved duration, dimensions, fps, codecs, pixel format, and audio stream.
- [ ] No clipping or unintended silence was found.
- [ ] Original source media remains unchanged.
- [ ] Final video, preview, script, storyboard, license log, and QA report are delivered.
